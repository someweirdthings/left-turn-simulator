What this sim isolates: signal timing required by the two specific paths in your diagrams. Both green in the ABD-style case; sequential green in the two-crossing Türkiye-style case. It does not claim every intersection in either country uses these phases. Other movements are represented by a fixed-duration phase, and only left-turn queues are simulated. Arrival events are identical in both scenarios, so differences arise from phase geometry and scheduling.
